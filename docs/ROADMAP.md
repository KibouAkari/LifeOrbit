# Roadmap

This is a living plan, **not** a list of completed features.

| Phase | Focus | Deliverable | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Foundation | Brand, documentation, contribution flow, release hygiene | Documentation prepared |
| 1 | Core experience | Sign-in, personal dashboard, tasks and notes | Planned |
| 2 | Planning | Calendar, reminders and upcoming deadlines | Planned |
| 3 | Life overview | Subscription tracking and renewals | Planned |
| 4 | Momentum | Daily/weekly challenges and progress | Planned |
| 5 | Polish | Responsive layout, accessibility, testing and demo | Planned |

## Prioritisation

Ship one coherent end-to-end experience before adding integrations. A reliable task flow with an excellent dashboard is more valuable in a hackathon than many unfinished screens.

## Demo story

1. Open LifeOrbit and sign in.
2. See your day at a glance.
3. Add a task, note and calendar item.
4. Review an upcoming subscription renewal.
5. Complete a challenge and watch progress update.

## Beyond the hackathon

Consider integrations, richer recurring reminders, user-controlled exports, improved analytics and accessibility refinements only after the core workflows are dependable.
