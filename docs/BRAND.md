# LifeOrbit brand direction

The name is **LifeOrbit** and the brand character is a simple, friendly,
cute planet with an orbital motif. Aim for memorable at 32px, not a complex
illustration. The repository currently includes `assets/logo.svg` and
`assets/icon.svg` as initial brand assets; these remain design candidates,
not final approved branding.

## Principles

- Simple geometric silhouette; one clear focal point.
- Soft teal/mint with a restrained blue accent.
- Rounded shapes, relaxed friendly expression, minimal details.
- Recognisable in one colour and at favicon size.
- No baked-in backgrounds, harsh shadows or excessive effects.
- Pair with crisp typography and plenty of whitespace.

## Accessibility

Text should meet WCAG AA contrast where practical; never rely on colour
alone to convey state. Keep a reduced-motion option for animations and
transitions. Decorative logo treatment should not replace accessible text.

Before finalising the brand, compare icon variants at 16, 32, 64 and 256px
and collect feedback from the hackathon team.
